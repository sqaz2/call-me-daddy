Why embarrass myself publicly?
Because “look what AI can do” is more interesting when you can also hear what it had to work with.

These ideas did not appear because AI existed in 2026. The archive proves that. It also proves that younger me was apparently comfortable saving files with names like roughcopy and then leaving them alive for more than a decade.

The new tools don't make the old recordings retroactively amazing. They let me take something unfinished, dated or buried on an old drive and ask a much more interesting question: what can I make from this now?
