NURIDE AWAY
FOUND A WAY BACK.

nuRide Away is the oldest recording in this project. I was more interested in recording and building songs than being the rapper in the room. I remember a friend who was into rapping hearing the idea and saying it wasn't really his style, but he could see how people would like that kind of music. That stuck with me.

Why the gap got so long
For a while, songs like this could have just stayed frozen on old drives.

After moving out west I bought a keyboard twice trying to get back into making music. Constant moving and downsizing meant I had to get rid of both of them.

The old recording survived anyway. Now affordable tools let me reopen the idea without first rebuilding the entire home studio I used to have.

The later old idea

ROUND THE BEND
BECAME CROCODILE SHOES.

By this point I had spent years writing, composing and learning recording. In 2010 I took music business and home recording while I was already composing in FL Studio and learning Pro Tools with an Mbox 2. Crocodile Shoes came later, more than a decade ago, with the Pokémon theme living rent-free somewhere in the inspiration.

Upload note: the file uploaded today as Crocodile Shoes.mp3 is byte-for-byte identical to the file named Crocodile Shoes 2015 special 2026 remix. I preserved both filenames in the archive folders, but the page does not pretend they are two different recordings.
Why embarrass myself publicly?
Because “look what AI can do” is more interesting when you can also hear what it had to work with.

The old recordings are not props invented for an AI argument. They are the actual surviving material: source vocals, rough copies and working ideas from years before the current tools existed.

The new versions do not erase that work. They show what happens when an old idea gets another chance after the studio, equipment and circumstances that produced it are gone.
