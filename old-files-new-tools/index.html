Why keep both?
The new version doesn't prove the old one was worthless. The old one proves the idea existed before the new tool did.

That's what makes this archive interesting to me. These aren't ideas generated out of nowhere in 2026. They are old recordings I can physically point to. Whatever I do with them now has a before.

AI changes what I can do with an old file, but it doesn't travel backward in time and make the old work disappear. I can keep the artifact, make a new interpretation, and let people hear the distance between them.
