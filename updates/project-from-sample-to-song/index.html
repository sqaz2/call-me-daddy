I built the DAW.
Then I made the beat.

I built my own DAW, sampled Satan’s.loan in its drum machine, used Grok to add a recognizable beat after the earlier audio was rejected, and took that seed into Suno. These are the two versions that came out of it.

The production diary follows the song and the software as they develop. Both Suno versions are linked there; the original seed’s local upload is still pending.
