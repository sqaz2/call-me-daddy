Live It Right.
The idea behind the song.

A song about waiting for instructions while your life keeps happening.

We can recognize what matters and still search for somebody qualified to tell us we are allowed to act on it. Preparing feels productive. Another explanation feels like progress. The years still pass.

The new song notes follow that contradiction through the hook, the 16-bar verse and a bridge about needing the confidence to stand when plans fall apart. The artwork turns the instructions themselves into a maze, beside a door that is already open.

The first song notes are now a full release page. Hear the recording, explore its fifteen-year history and read the artist’s note about bringing it to life with AI.
