## Opener and audience evidence

The title version is an editorial starting point, not an asserted popularity winner. No trustworthy cross-listener aggregate analytics source is connected. Audience reordering is disabled; browser storage and repeat play events cannot enable it.

Before an audience-selected opener can be implemented and enabled, the evidence must provide all of the following:

1. At least 100 deduplicated, qualified listeners for **each** of the three main versions.
2. The same observation window, spanning at least 14 days for **each** version.
3. Comparable exposure, such as balanced or randomized presentation.
4. A clear statistical preference, not merely the largest raw total. Ties and uncertain results keep the editorial opener.
5. Validated aggregate provenance, bot/internal/test filtering, and a documented deduplication method. Repeated plays do not create new listeners.
6. `end-this-cycle` is excluded from every candidate comparison.

These are minimum eligibility requirements, not a promise that 100 listeners determines a winner. Missing, malformed, stale or unverifiable evidence must keep the editorial order. No listener counts or popularity labels are displayed.

Explicit version links and deliberate listener selections remain authoritative. The bonus is shareable and playable, but never chosen as the ordinary release/radio opener. If a listener has personally excluded all three main versions, radio omits this song rather than substituting its bonus.
