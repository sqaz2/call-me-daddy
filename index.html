Explicit archive · Sqaz

SQAZ — the 2007 tape survived.

Kill You came from a completely different point in my life: teenage shock rap, recorded at 17, then brought back as a 2026 remix. The interesting part now isn't just the aggression. It's what was underneath it.

Before I stopped making rap music—not an oxymoron—I was about 20 or 21 when I counted more than 700 instrumentals and pieces of music I had made. Only a few instrumentals are public now, mostly on SoundCloud; the archive is much bigger than the surviving links make it look.
