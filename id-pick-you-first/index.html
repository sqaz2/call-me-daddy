A RELATIONSHIP TEST ESCALATED

I’D PICK

It started with one of those impossible little questions couples ask each other. Then somebody brought a booger into it. Somehow that became the most sincere line in the song.

01 · The ridiculous question

WOULD YOU STILL
LOVE ME IF…?

It came from that specific kind of relationship conversation where the hypothetical keeps getting more absurd and the answer is still supposed to prove something real.

“If I was a caterpillar, would you still love me?”
“What if I was a booger?”
02 · The answer
“If you were a booger,
I’d pick you first.”

It is gross. It is stupid. It is also a complete answer: even in the dumbest possible version of you, I am still choosing you before everybody else.

Why the joke works

THE PUNCHLINE IS ALSO THE LOVE SONG.

The hypothetical is ridiculous, but the reassurance underneath it is not. The line works because it refuses to become elegant. It stays gross and still manages to say, “I choose you.”

The moving background

THE SONG NEVER STOPS MAKING THE TEST WEIRDER.

The background clip leans into another absurd romantic image from the song — becoming a bubble in somebody’s bath. The point is not realism. It is commitment to the bit.

How it became a release

RIDICULOUS → CATCHY → WEIRDLY SINCERE.

That is the whole route. A throwaway relationship-test joke had enough emotional logic underneath it to survive being turned into a full song. The remastered version keeps the humor, but the reason the hook sticks is that the answer is actually affectionate.
