Why this exists

In the Red Deer Rants and Raves group, Wendy Lichti posted that Superstore had about eighty carts left — people keep taking them, more are on order, and unless you follow someone to their car you aren’t shopping. She left. Comments turned into wheel locks, magnets, and civic grief. That felt like a song. Commit note on the upload: the superstore effect (punch buggie red deer).

This page is not punching down at people who are unhoused, and it is not mocking Wendy for being annoyed in a grocery line. Cart theft and store logistics are messy. The joke is turning a local Facebook inventory crisis into a full satirical release.

The concern is real. The hooded-prophet-outside-Superstore cinematic is intentional. The affection for Red Deer community posts is sincere.
