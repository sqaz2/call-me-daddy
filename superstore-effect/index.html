Why this exists

In the Red Deer Rants and Raves group, Wendy Lichti posted that Superstore had about eighty carts left — people keep taking them, more are on order, and unless you follow someone to their car you aren’t shopping. She left. Comments turned into wheel locks, magnets, and civic grief. That felt like a song. Commit note on the upload: the superstore effect (punch buggie red deer).
