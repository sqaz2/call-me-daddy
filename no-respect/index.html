Behind the song

Two countries, same hunger

By October 24, 2026, William J. Crowell in Alberta and Dogg Knows in South Africa were working out how to share a gangsta rap track. “Two Countries, Same Hunger” was one of the ideas on the table. The title that stuck came from Dogg talking to his son.

In William’s verse, a set of keys measures how much his life has changed. He once had the keys to the dealership. Now he’s eating at the Mustard Seed and building a studio in the same van he sleeps in.

On October 25, William was going line by line through Dogg’s verse, trying to understand how his words moved between languages. Dogg starts out chasing cash. Then he sits down to write, thinks about his boy, and can’t finish a verse. A few bars later he’s buying cake for his son’s first birthday.

The handoff

“yo dogg-knows, who'd you write for /all those nights on your own?”

By the end of October, William had Dogg’s recording as a single audio file with the voice and beat together. On November 2, he settled on the question that closes his verse. The intro says “I wake up lonely.” William remembers a winter night too cold to sleep. When Dogg comes in with “all them nights alone,” he gets to tell us who he was thinking about.
