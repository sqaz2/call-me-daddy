Why split one song?

SAME MUSIC.
DIFFERENT ENERGY.

The experiment is about separating emotional ingredients without pretending the difficult ones don't exist.

The shadow

Put the poison in its own container.

HELL HAS PEOPLE TOO holds the envy, comparison, resentment, insecurity and conditional support. Instead of blending those feelings into everything, the song gives them somewhere specific to live.

The climb

Keep what helps you move.

Find Your People takes the same underlying lesson in the other direction: stop shrinking for the wrong room, recognize genuine support, and move toward people who can celebrate your growth.

The bridge

Remove the words.

The instrumental sits between both songs because it belongs to neither emotional interpretation. It is the common fabric: proof that the same musical foundation can carry opposite psychological framing.

Your route

Direction changes context.

Start dark and the experience can feel like moving out of resentment toward connection. Start positive and the same sequence becomes a reminder of what you're choosing to leave behind. The listener supplies the narrative.
