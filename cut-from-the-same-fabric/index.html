Why I split it

ONE SONG WAS
TRYING TO BE TWO.

The original idea had a useful point buried inside a pile of resentment. Instead of pretending those were the same thing, I gave each one its own song.

Same source

I kept the music related on purpose.

These aren't random songs that happen to share a theme. They were remixed from the same family of sound so you can hear how much the words change the meaning.

Negative cut

Let the ugly stuff say what it came to say.

HELL HAS PEOPLE TOO gets the jealousy, comparison, resentment, insecurity and fake support. It doesn't need to leak into the other song anymore.

Positive cut

Keep the lesson without carrying the poison.

Find Your People keeps the part I actually want to live by: don't shrink for the wrong room, and notice the people who are genuinely happy to see you grow.

Your order

The same three tracks tell two different stories.

Start negative and it moves toward something better. Start positive and the last song sounds more like a warning about what you're leaving behind.
