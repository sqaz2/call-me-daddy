Origin · September 3, 2012

The poem

This poem predates the Sep 2026 Suno/Canva release by fourteen years. The song grew from these lines — shy/wise, travellers optics, planter of root.

a flower in the wind with destination to the sky
a path that shows promise, only a beautiful sight
a travellers optics show a danger on route
what comes down is not always that which took to the sky, sweeping shute
but it is not worth regretting thy planter of root
for the seeds spread the land
and the the cycle renews
shy was the flower whom takes good ol' time to bloom
wise was wind whom chose what to do
-
Will Crowell 9/3/12

A Facebook repost of this poem existed around September 2026.

The story

The September 3, 2012 poem above is the root — shy/wise, travellers optics, planter of root — fourteen years before this page. Borrowed time. A flower pushing through concrete. Satan's Loan holds the chorus line — nothing leaves the way it came — and scatters the rest across blood, bones, and dirt under the miles. The lyric cast shifted from an earlier Shahar was the flower / Rhiz was the wind opening (still audible on the intermediate Suno cut) to the Shy was the flower / wise was wind sheet on this page — and from a brief “Satan's lone” spelling to Satan's loan.

The release landed over four days in early September 2026 — domain, direction, lyrics, public cuts, Canva music video, and this highlight page. Sound explored a short rap / boom-bap preview, then a rock / GTA-flavored full-length cut, before the Canva MV soundtrack (~2:13) became the release audio.
