FROM THE SENT FOLDER
Date
May 22, 2010
Subject
hi andrew
Attachment
tims.wav

A rough recording.
A piece of the song's history.

I started This Is My Story in 2009. In 2010, I sent a rough recording to Andrew in an email. The file was called tims.wav—short for “This Is My Story.”

In that email, I was asking about a microphone for loud vocals. That rough copy was still there, attached to a message in my sent folder.

In 2026, I went looking through my old emails with ChatGPT and found it again.

These are new versions of a song I started in 2009.

Now that recording has become two special 2026 AI remixes: the remix and the gospel remix. Both come from the song I recorded back then.

An old song. A new life. And the story is still mine.

— William
