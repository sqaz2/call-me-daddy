From the comments to the hook

Everybody’s their own
biggest fan.

A post asked people to speak up if they had music that was hot but hadn’t reached a big audience. My reply: “everybody thinks they’re hot.”

That became the title and the hook. The song takes a swing at the press-kit millionaires, the big announcements, and the comment sections where everyone has something to promote and nobody stops to hear the next person.

“Funny how the whole department fits inside your kitchen.”

And yes, there’s a joke in putting out a song about everyone putting out songs. I’m in the same comment section. Okay, okay — let it play.
