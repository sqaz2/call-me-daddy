WHAT’S UNDERNEATH

The hurt doesn’t
stay inside.

Feeling shitty can come out sideways: in the comments, in the joke that cuts too deep, in the way somebody else becomes the problem because you already feel bad about yourself.

Everybody Else Less sits inside that spiral. The fictional narrator isn’t happy with their own reflection, but takes the feeling out on everyone around them. The hurt explains where the spite comes from. It doesn’t make the damage disappear.

“You don’t like me?
I don’t like me”
