How this happened

“Novelty is my favourite
thing in the world.”

Suno offered to make a song from a picture. It was new, so of course I tried it. It asked my name. I was looking at myself and typed “Big Dick Willy.”

What came back was a quiet, light country-ish song. I spent an hour or two in Generative.download, my mobile DAW: separating the stems locally, editing the vocals and beat, widening the sound, and working on the mix.

Then I exported it and put it back into Suno. The next result had me thinking, “Wow, I’m kind of jamming to this.” Enough that I decided to record myself feeling the song.
