THE STORY SO FAR

I made the tool.
Then used it
to make music.

MusicSubject × Call Me Daddy

I built my own DAW at generative.download. Then I sampled my last song, Satan’s.loan, in its drum machine.

Suno wasn’t accepting the earlier audio. I used Grok to add a recognizable beat, then used that seed in Suno. These are the two songs that came out of it.

Incredible.

I’ll be updating this page as I build onto the song and keep developing my DAW. The song isn’t finished, and neither is the tool.
