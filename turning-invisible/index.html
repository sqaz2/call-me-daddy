A note from William

I just remixed
an email.

Yes, you read that correctly.
This is a remixed email you’re listening to.

AI mixLike a remix, but with AI.

I connected my email to ChatGPT and asked it to find my old poetry. It found lyrics I’d emailed years ago—including “She’s Turning Invisible,” sitting in a text attachment from 2015.

Then I brought in something from a completely different song: an instrumental I made in FL Studio back in 2009.

So this is two different pieces of my past coming together. Words from one song, with a bit of AI help changing them, and music from another. I brought that into Suno, gave it my prompts, and let it take over a little. Then I changed things a couple of times to get closer to the sound I was looking for.

And it’s really interesting hearing something this familiar become something new.

When you make an instrumental, you listen to it so many times. Over and over while you’re building it, changing it, and trying to get it right. Eventually it becomes ingrained in your memory of that time. That year.

I don’t remember much else about making this one. But I remember the music.

In 2007, I was writing. I wasn’t doing the instrumental part yet, but I already had it in my head that I wanted to do the whole thing. By 2009, I was making my own instrumentals. I think I made quite a few around then.

Now it’s 2026, and I’m putting those pieces together in a way I couldn’t back then.

An old instrumental. Lyrics recovered from an email. My prompts, my changes, and AI helping me hear another possibility.
