The origin story

THE WORKFLOW WAS LOSING TO THE PURITY TEST.

Disliking AI output is an opinion. Needing flyers and signage for a business, then starting by disqualifying useful tools because they might contain AI, is where the logic became funny enough to turn into a song.

What I heard

Someone wanted collage-style apps for flyers and posters, remembered PicMonkey from before AI was common, and wanted to avoid tools that now incorporate AI.

What my brain heard

“Please help me make the job harder, but make sure the software passes a technology purity inspection first.”

Normal escalation

Screen recording → argument → lyrics → dubstep → Facebook group.
