01 / PRESSURE NEEDS A VOICE

I Need That Sound

I Need That Sound is Stomp Clamp rebuilt. I wrote it from the middle of the climb—not after everything worked out. Money gets thin. The work keeps going. The plan is still alive. Sometimes I don’t need a victory speech. I need one loud voice while I’m still in the dirt.

THE PERSON IN THE PROCESS

The tools changed the sound.
They didn’t live the life.

I’m making these while working on websites, music, and anything else that might help me move forward. A lot of it happens from a phone, inside a van, while I’m trying to build my way into a safer and more stable life.

The songs are not a polished success story written from the other side. They are notes from inside it: anger without surrender, loneliness without pretending I no longer need people, and ambition that keeps showing up even when I’m exhausted.

Suno is part of how I audition different versions of the music. I decide what stays, what feels false, and which sound carries the words honestly. The technology is in the process. The history, choices, and reasons for making it are mine.

I’m still here.
I’m still building.
I need that sound.
