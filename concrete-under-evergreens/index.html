Why this exists

A community post reported two small wheelbarrows of leftover concrete beneath trees in Big Dipper Park. The city was notified. I made a song about the scale of the reaction.

This page does not condone dumping concrete under trees, and the person who reported it is not the target. Parks deserve care. Trees deserve care. The joke is the wildly disproportionate musical response.

The concern is real. The cinematic background is fictional. The affection for Lacombe is sincere.
