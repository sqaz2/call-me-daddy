Why this exists

In the Red Deer Rants and Raves group, Wendy Lichti posted that Superstore was down to about eighty carts — staff said people keep taking them, more are on order, and unless you’re willing to follow someone to their car, you’re not shopping. She left. The comments immediately turned into wheel locks, magnets, security ideas, and civic grief. That felt like a song.

THE RANT WAS REAL.
THE CHORUS IS THE JOKE.

This page is not punching down at people who are unhoused, and it is not mocking Wendy for being annoyed in a grocery line. Cart theft and store logistics are messy. The joke is turning a local Facebook inventory crisis into a full satirical release.

The concern is real. The parking-lot cinematic is fictional. The affection for Red Deer community posts is sincere.
