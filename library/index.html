What does the match score mean?

The score is the share of your search terms found in a recording’s catalogue tags or text. Mood and genre terms match tags; other words also search titles, notes and published lyrics. Missing terms are shown. Exact song titles appear first. It is not a quality rating or a prediction of placement. Filters are strict: unknown information does not pass a selected filter. Try “no vocals” for identified instrumentals or “-comedy” to exclude a tag.
