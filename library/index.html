What does the match score mean?

The score is the share of your search terms found in a recording’s catalogue tags or text. Mood and genre terms match documented tags. Title fragments, alternate names, versions, notes and published lyrics are searchable too. Complete matches appear first; when none fit, closest matches show the missing terms. Exact song titles appear first. It is not a quality rating or a prediction of placement. Filters are strict: unknown information does not pass a selected filter. Try “no vocals” for identified instrumentals or “-comedy” to exclude a tag.
