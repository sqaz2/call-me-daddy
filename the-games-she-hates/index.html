Same poem. Three recordings.

September 25, 2012

Fourteen years
between the lines.

Written by Will Crowell in 2012. Turned into music in 2026, with the original words kept intact.

Original post on Facebook ↗
Read the original poem +
I sense she is closed, she opens though I am a stranger
She tells she does not like games, but she reveals that's what attracts her
She says she was good, but she seems that she is unsure
She reveals there was pain, I can tell she's been hurt
At 26, she dresses in a blue top with dress pants
she does not dress to attract the baddest of men
I agree
Her hair is dark, her skin is fair, her words are spoken with
intelligence
A girl such as her so rare
She is unlike many of her friends
But she does not know what kind of man she wants
That is tragic in my eyes
So she plays the games she says she hates
and it's a test for men
this is how she lives her life
I wish to end this cycle
Love is a beautiful feeling
Just be yourself, and do not hold back
your behavior is beautiful by nature
-
Will Crowell September 25/12
