MusicSubject for Android

App privacy.

Effective October 14, 2026. This notice covers the MusicSubject Android player, package com.musicsubject.player.

What stays on your device

The app keeps saved-song choices, a cached catalogue, and the last playback queue and position in its local storage. These are not synced to an account. There is no account sign-in, advertising SDK or analytics SDK in this version of the Android app.

Internet requests

The app connects to callmedaddy.musicsubject.com to retrieve the song catalogue, artwork and audio. Those requests necessarily send your IP address and request information to the website and its hosting provider, Cloudflare. Hosting and security systems may process access logs to deliver content, diagnose errors and protect the service. This notice does not promise that hosting logs contain no personal information.

Permissions and external links

The app uses internet access and Android’s foreground media playback service to stream music, including while the player is in the background. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. Song stories and website links open in your browser, where the website’s own storage and third-party links may apply. Sharing opens the device share sheet; information you choose to send is handled by the receiving app.

Removing local data

Clear MusicSubject’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app to remove its saved songs, cached catalogue and playback state from your device. This does not remove hosting-provider logs or data held by other apps you used to share links.

Contact and updates

For app support and privacy questions, contact the MusicSubject project through its issue tracker. Issues are public: do not include private personal information. This notice will be updated when the app’s data practices change.
