What I’m asking

Try it. Keep it installed.
Stay in the test for 14 days.

Google requires at least 12 people to remain opted into the closed test for 14 continuous days before I can apply for a public release. Use the app during that time and share what you find. Joining the group by itself does not start that requirement.

There’s no purchase or public review required. A website listen or a direct APK installation is welcome, but it does not count as joining the Google Play closed test.

A couple of things to know

Google Play says I’m not eligible.

Check that you joined the tester group and that the Play Store is using that same Google account. If you were in the earlier internal test, leave that test before opting into the closed test. Access can take time to update. If it still fails, send me a note.

I already installed MusicSubject from the website.

You still need to join the group and opt into the Google Play closed test. Play may show Open if you already have the same version. Keep the app installed so your local favourites stay with you.

What does the app need?

An Android phone running Android 8.0 or later and an internet connection for streaming. The music player needs no account, but Google requires a Google account for test enrollment. Favourites are stored on your device; offline song downloads are not included.

Can I listen on an iPhone or a computer?

Yes—the music website works without joining this Android test.
