Add MusicSubject to your home screen. Open your songs, alternate versions and stories in their own app window.

Start listening →

Start a song, then use your phone’s playback controls while you’re away.

Install from your browser

Android: open in Chrome. Tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen → Install.

iPhone: open in Safari. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

Inside Facebook or Instagram? Use its menu to open this page in your phone’s browser first.

Keep the music with you

Press play before switching apps or locking your screen. Keep the app open in the background.

Songs stream over the internet. Offline song downloads aren’t included yet. Phone battery settings, other audio apps and browser restrictions can still interrupt playback.
