How the idea moved

REACTION → LAUGH
→ ARMANDO.

The interesting part is the chain of decisions. Nothing started with “write a song called Armando.” One recording created another idea, and I kept following it.

01
Record

The video came first.

I recorded my reaction. The laugh was incidental—just part of what happened while the camera was rolling.

02
Notice

The laugh became source material.

I isolated the laugh instead of treating the reaction video as a finished object. That little fragment became something I could arrange.

03
Transform

Build music around the accident.

The laugh became an intro, then a recurring sample. I started dropping fragments over and into the beat rather than simply pasting the whole clip onto a song.

04
Edit

Keep chopping until something wins.

I’ve been cutting sections, preserving vocals that almost got lost at splice points, repeating the laugh only where it earns the repetition, and testing alternate instrumental directions.
