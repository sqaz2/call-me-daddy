Where it started

ONE LAUGH
GOT OUT OF HAND.

The original reaction. Before the song existed.

More story

Reaction → laugh → sample → Armando. I tried a few directions, chopped them up, and kept iterating. The version above won because the question finally gave the whole joke a point: did Armando die after you held his beer?
