How the idea moved

THE REACTION
BECAME THE SOURCE.

The useful part was not sitting down with a finished song idea. It was noticing that something spontaneous inside another recording had musical value, then following it.

01
Record

The video came first.

I recorded my reaction to a video. At that point, the laugh was just part of the reaction—not a planned vocal or a song component.

02
Notice

The laugh became the interesting part.

I heard something in my own reaction worth keeping, so I isolated the laugh from the rest of the recording and treated it like source material.

03
Transform

Turn the moment into music.

Instead of leaving the laugh as a clip, I used the idea as the beginning of a song. The accidental reaction became intentional production.

04
Edit

Keep chopping until something wins.

The laugh has been layered, repeated, faded and dropped into different parts of the beat. Two Suno generations came out of the experiment too. I’m keeping those as sketches while I decide which musical DNA is actually worth carrying forward.
