Something has come home.

A creature has learned her laugh, borrowed her face, and walked into her home. It knows enough to get inside. Her wife is starting to notice what it got wrong.

Where AI comes in

Sometimes I write the lyrics myself. For Her Story, I had a beat from over a decade ago and wanted to hear it with words. I came up with the story idea and used ChatGPT to write the lyrics.

I thought about replacing the words later. I’m still not a fan of the lyrics, but I love the way this recording sounds. That’s why I kept it.

I learned her laugh from the clips on her phone,
Practised “I’m back” till it sounded like home.
Jaw wouldn’t fit, had to crack it in place,
Spent half of the night getting used to her face.

Milk in the fridge, little list on the door,
Things she still needed. She won’t anymore.
Her wife heard the lock, called her name from upstairs,
I answered too quickly. She stopped halfway there.

Said, “Long day?” I nodded. She reached for the light,
I smiled with the teeth I’d put in wrong that night.
She said, “You look sick.” I said, “Something I ate.”
Kept checking her watch like I might be running late.

Then something beneath my shirt started to crawl—
Her knuckles pressed through as I stood in the hall.
She whispered her name. I said, “Come over here.”
Her voice in my stomach said, “Don’t come near.”
What the ending means Spoilers+

The borrowed face invites her wife closer. The voice inside warns her away. For that final line, the woman the creature swallowed takes her story back.
