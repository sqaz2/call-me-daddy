Original staging notes

Play the existing recording continuously. RECORDING cues reproduce the original verse word for word. Actors mouth the dialogue in sync; Ellen silently mouths Mara’s name where the verse describes it. Shot lengths follow the recorded phrases. Keep effects beneath the vocal. MARA is the woman in the phone clips. ELLEN is her wife. THE CREATURE wears Mara’s face. The same performer plays Mara and the creature.

INT. DISUSED WASHROOM - NIGHT

A phone leans against a stained mirror. In a home video, MARA laughs. A gold watch hangs loose on her wrist. She taps its face twice, then reaches affectionately toward the person filming.

RECORDING (V.O.)

I learned her laugh from the clips on her phone,

A thumb drags the video back. The laugh repeats. An unseen figure tries it. A rough breath, almost a cough. It rewinds again.

RECORDING (V.O.)

Practised “I’m back” till it sounded like home.

In the mirror: only a mouth. It rehearses the words. The lips move too widely the first time. On the next attempt, they barely move at all.

RECORDING (V.O.)

Jaw wouldn’t fit, had to crack it in place,

The lower jaw hangs off-centre. A hand presses it sideways. A wet CRACK. The mouth finally closes.

RECORDING (V.O.)

Spent half of the night getting used to her face.

The figure leans fully into view. Mara's face. A raw fold of skin sits beneath one ear. It tucks the fold out of sight, then fastens the gold watch. On the phone, the real Mara is still laughing.

INT. HOUSE - KITCHEN - NIGHT

ELLEN, Mara's wife, pours milk into a mug. A second mug waits beside it.

RECORDING (V.O.)

Milk in the fridge, little list on the door,

She returns the milk. As the fridge closes, a handwritten list comes into view: BREAD. COFFEE. SHAMPOO.

RECORDING (V.O.)

Things she still needed. She won’t anymore.

A small heart follows the last item. Ellen takes her mug upstairs. The second mug stays where it is.

INT. HOUSE - FRONT HALL - CONTINUOUS

RECORDING (V.O.)

Her wife heard the lock, called her name from upstairs,

A key turns. THE CREATURE steps inside, wearing Mara's face and coat. Ellen appears at the top of the stairs. She mouths: Mara?

RECORDING (V.O.)

I answered too quickly. She stopped halfway there.

Before Ellen finishes, the creature mouths its rehearsed greeting. The response is immediate, ready-made. Ellen stops halfway down. She studies its face. The creature stands with one hand still on the door handle. After a moment, Ellen comes down the remaining steps.

RECORDING (V.O.)

Said, “Long day?” I nodded. She reached for the light,

She mouths the question. It nods once. She reaches past it and switches on the hall light.

RECORDING (V.O.)

I smiled with the teeth I’d put in wrong that night.

The creature smiles. Two teeth face the wrong way. One sits too high, its root showing above the gum. Ellen's fingers stay on the switch.

RECORDING (V.O.)

She said, “You look sick.” I said, “Something I ate.”

Ellen mouths: You look sick. The creature swallows, then gives the recorded reply. Its smile stays in place. Ellen looks from its mouth to its eyes.

RECORDING (V.O.)

Kept checking her watch like I might be running late.

The creature raises its wrist. Mara's gold watch. It taps the face twice, exactly as she did in the video. Lowers it. Checks it again. The second hand has stopped. Ellen watches the borrowed gesture.

RECORDING (V.O.)

Then something beneath my shirt started to crawl—

A ridge travels sideways beneath the creature's shirt. Both its hands remain visible: one on the banister, the other hanging beside its thigh. Something else is moving under the fabric.

RECORDING (V.O.)

Her knuckles pressed through as I stood in the hall.

A button pulls free. Through the opening, four knuckles press outward from beneath its skin. A fingernail draws a short red line from the inside. Ellen sets her mug on the stair. Her eyes stay on the movement.

RECORDING (V.O.)

She whispered her name. I said, “Come over here.”

Ellen mouths Mara's name. This time she is looking at its stomach. The creature offers its hand, copying the tender reach from the home video. It mouths: Come over here. Ellen's hand starts toward it.

RECORDING (V.O.)

Her voice in my stomach said, “Don’t come near.”

On the final warning, the creature's mouth is firmly closed. The shape in its stomach pushes upward with the effort of speaking. Ellen hears where the voice is coming from. Her fingers stop inches from its open palm.

Inside Her Story

Everything is where
she left it.

Milk in the fridge. A list on the door. Her wife upstairs, hearing a familiar key in the lock.

What walks through the door has been rehearsing. It has studied the clips on her phone, practised her greeting, and spent hours getting comfortable in her face. It knows enough to get inside.

Her Story puts you inside that performance. The narrator talks about a jaw that will not fit and teeth put in wrong as casually as somebody describing a long day. Meanwhile, her wife is trying to understand why the person she knows feels unfamiliar.

The horror gathers in the small mistakes. An answer comes too quickly. A smile reveals too much. Even checking a watch becomes a copied human habit: a little gesture meant to make the disguise believable.

Things she still needed.
She won’t anymore.

From the original verse

That shopping list is a future interrupted. Someone expected to come back, finish the errands, and keep living with the person upstairs. Something else has arrived in her place.

After listening · SpoilersWhat the ending reveals+

Her face has been taken.
Her voice has not.

The movement beneath the shirt changes everything. This creature has consumed her as well as impersonated her—but something of the woman is still inside, pushing back.

Whether she is physically alive or surviving through something supernatural stays unresolved. What matters is what she does with the voice she has left.

The borrowed face invites her wife closer. The voice inside warns her away.

“Come over here.”
“Don’t come near.”

One body delivers two opposite intentions. The creature wants another victim. The woman wants her wife to live.

For that final line, it becomes her story again.
