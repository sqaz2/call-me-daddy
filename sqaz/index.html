2007 · AGE 17

Where it came from

By high school, I had blocked out a lot of memories because I had to survive. Life was painful and unsatisfying, and Kill You came out of that. At the time, it was a warning to other people. Sqaz was more than the username I had started with: I identified with it as an alter person in me.

I had written the lyrics beforehand. The recording took about six hours in Audacity, using a broken USB microphone and what I had taught myself. I was figuring out how to turn the words into a finished song.

Some of the people who liked it also listened to ICP. That was an overlap in listeners; ICP wasn’t the influence behind the song.

SOUNDCLICK · MP3 PLAYERS · PICZO

People passed it around

I first put it on SoundClick, and I remember it reaching the second page of the rap listings. Later it was on pwn.piczo.com, where people left comments.

A friend’s older sister heard it on his MP3 player and told him it was the only good song on there. I remember people giving me props as we moved between classes. Those moments mattered to me, and the attention went to my head.

I became a teenage alcoholic, got into fights and pushed friends away. Sometimes, in a group of a dozen or so people, I’d be asked to perform the rap. My buddy Zach knew the words, and I’d look to him when I was too drunk to remember them.

TAKING IT DOWN

“I didn’t want to be known as that.”

Eventually, I deleted the song because I didn’t want that identity to be what people knew me for.

Looking back, the part I appreciate most is how much other people did for it. Friends listened, shared the file and remembered the words. Without their support, this song wouldn’t have survived.

2026

Hearing it again

Bringing the recording back with AI let me hear something I made at seventeen in a different way. Kill You and Twokill now have separate releases, both kept here under Sqaz.

I’m still making music. I wanted to bring this part of the story back with some distance, and give credit to the people who helped it survive.

Before the song: the name and the writing

I created Sqaz as an online alias around 2000–2001. The name came from the number 1 spelled out with keyboard letters. I also built and promoted a game-hacking website that I remember drawing roughly 1,000 hits a day.

At thirteen or fourteen, I was sent to an adolescent treatment centre in Halifax, Nova Scotia. I started writing poetry regularly there; it was also where I was introduced to drugs and alcohol. The writing continued long before I had a way to record it.

By 2010, I was studying music business and home recording, composing in FL Studio and learning Pro Tools with an Mbox 2.
